Privacy Policy

Effective June 1, 2026

Prometheus does not collect personal data. We only collect limited anonymous telemetry to help maintain the app and fix issues.

Personal Data

Personal data is information that can identify you directly or indirectly, such as your name, email address, precise location, account identifiers, or content tied to your identity. Prometheus does not collect this kind of information.

Anonymous Telemetry

Prometheus may collect anonymous technical details such as device type, device locale, iOS version, app version, crash information, and basic performance diagnostics. This information is not used to identify you.

Collection and Use

Anonymous telemetry is used only to maintain Prometheus, diagnose crashes, understand issues, and improve app reliability. We do not sell personal data because we do not collect personal data.

Third-Party Websites

Prometheus is a web browser. Websites you visit may collect data under their own privacy policies and practices. Prometheus does not control those websites, and this policy does not apply to data they collect.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at prometheusbrowser@icloud.com.
